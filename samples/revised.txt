SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 15, 2025,
by and between Acme Corporation ("Provider") and Gamma Technologies ("Client").

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
The Provider shall deliver software consulting and development services as
described in Exhibit A attached hereto. Services will be performed remotely
or at the Provider's offices during normal business hours.

2. TERM
This Agreement shall commence on March 15, 2025 and continue for a
period of twenty-four (24) months, unless terminated earlier in accordance
with Section 6.

3. COMPENSATION
Client shall pay Provider a monthly fee of $15,000, due on the first
business day of each month. Late payments shall incur interest at a
rate of 1.0% per month.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party agrees to keep confidential all proprietary information
received from the other party during the term of this Agreement.
Confidential information shall remain protected for two (2) years
following termination of this Agreement.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work product created under this Agreement shall be owned by the Client
upon full payment.

6. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 60 days written notice.
Upon termination, Client shall pay for all services rendered through
the termination date. Provider shall return all Client materials within
10 business days of termination.
