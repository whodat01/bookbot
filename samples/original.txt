SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2025,
by and between Acme Corporation ("Provider") and Beta Industries ("Client").

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
The Provider shall deliver software consulting services as described in
Exhibit A attached hereto. Services will be performed at the Provider's
offices during normal business hours.

2. TERM
This Agreement shall commence on January 1, 2025 and continue for a
period of twelve (12) months, unless terminated earlier in accordance
with Section 5.

3. COMPENSATION
Client shall pay Provider a monthly fee of $10,000, due on the first
business day of each month. Late payments shall incur interest at a
rate of 1.5% per month.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party agrees to keep confidential all proprietary information
received from the other party during the term of this Agreement.

5. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice.
Upon termination, Client shall pay for all services rendered through
the termination date.
